    Opening

    • Control the center with a pawn (e4/d4/e5/d5) in your first few moves.
    • Develop knights before bishops — they have fewer good squares.
    • Castle early, usually within your first 6–8 moves.
    • Don't move the same piece twice before finishing development.

    Tactics

    • Before every move, check: does this hang a piece?
    • Look for forks, pins, and skewers on your opponent's last move.
    • A piece that attacks two things at once is usually winning material.
    • Practice daily in the Puzzles tab — pattern recognition compounds fast.

    Endgame

    • King activity matters most once queens are off the board.
    • Push passed pawns — they only get more dangerous with fewer pieces on the board.
    • In king + pawn endings, opposition (facing kings with one square between) is key.
    • Rooks belong behind passed pawns — yours or your opponent's.

    Mindset

    • Slow down in critical positions — most blunders happen on "quick" moves.
    • Review your losses. Losses teach more than wins.
    • A daily streak beats a single long session — consistency builds intuition.
    • It's fine to lose to the practice bot — that's what it's there for.
